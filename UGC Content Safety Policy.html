1. Scope

This policy applies to content submitted, created, processed, saved, shared, or reported through PrismPop, including source images, facial images, videos, prompts, masks, generated images, generated videos, previews, templates, profiles, messages, and shared materials where those features are available. It applies to private and shared content when review is needed to provide the Service, prevent abuse, protect rights, comply with law, or respond to a report.

PrismPop is operated by TABLINK MEDIA LIMITED. The User Agreement and Terms & Conditions, together with the Privacy Policy, also apply to use of the Service.

2. Content and conduct that are not allowed

You must not use the Service to upload, create, process, publish, or share content that:

  1. sexually exploits, sexualises, endangers, or abuses a child;
  2. contains pornography, sexualised nudity, sexual services, or non-consensual intimate imagery;
  3. depicts or encourages credible threats, graphic violence, terrorism, self-harm, or serious criminal activity;
  4. harasses, bullies, threatens, targets, or promotes hatred against a person or protected group;
  5. reveals private information or infringes privacy, publicity, copyright, trademark, confidentiality, or other rights;
  6. impersonates a person or organisation, or uses synthetic media to deceive or cause harm;
  7. facilitates fraud, identity theft, malware, illegal activity, or evasion of safety or access controls; or
  8. is spam, a scam, or otherwise prohibited by applicable law, an app store rule, or the User Agreement.

3. Consent and rights

You must own or have permission to use every image, video, voice, likeness, prompt, and other material that you submit or share. Before using another person’s likeness, facial features, voice, identity, or private material, obtain that person’s valid permission and any other authorization required by law. A public image or a previous upload does not by itself establish consent for a new generation or publication.

Do not remove or obscure a required synthetic-media label, provenance marker, rights notice, or watermark. A claim of artistic intent, adult consent, or an AI label does not make prohibited content acceptable.

4. Safety screening

For relevant image inputs and AI-generated image outputs, the Service uses Alibaba Cloud Content Moderation’s Image Review Enhanced API (ImageModeration) for image safety detection. This screening is intended to identify risk signals before an image is processed or delivered. A provider response is not proof that content is lawful, consensual, or free of third-party rights issues.

The confirmed screening scope is image input and image output. This policy does not claim that text or video is covered by the same check, and it does not promise that automated screening will identify every prohibited item.

5. Reports, complaints, and appeals

Use the in-app Report action where it is available. If no suitable in-app action is available, contact prismpop8893@gmail.com. Include the content or account identifier, a short description, and the privacy, safety, intellectual-property, or other rights basis for the report. Do not send unnecessary sensitive material.

People depicted in content, rights holders, parents or guardians, and other affected persons may use the same contact to report unauthorized or harmful content. You may also contact us if you believe an enforcement decision is incorrect. We may request information needed to verify a report or complaint. We do not promise a particular response time or outcome.

6. Possible actions

Depending on the content, context, severity, repeat behaviour, legal requirements, and available evidence, we may reject an upload or generation request, withhold or remove a result, limit sharing or discovery, restrict a feature, request additional information, suspend or terminate access, preserve limited records where legally permitted or required, or refer a serious concern to an appropriate authority. We may act without prior notice where permitted by law or needed to protect people or the Service.

7. Children and urgent safety

Sexualised content involving a minor is prohibited regardless of whether it is real or AI-generated and regardless of claimed consent or artistic intent. Do not use the Service to identify, target, contact, expose, or exploit a child. The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age, or such higher age as required by applicable law.

If a person is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Use the contact above for a safety or rights report and include only the information needed for us to investigate.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, risks, laws, store rules, or safety controls change. The published version and date will be shown in the Application’s legal links.
