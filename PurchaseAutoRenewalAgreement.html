If the purchase screen identifies a product as auto-renewable, it will renew for the disclosed billing period at the disclosed renewal price unless you cancel using the applicable cancellation method in Section 4 below before the next renewal. The store may apply its own billing retries, grace periods, account holds, taxes, currency conversion, and price-change notice rules.

Any separate auto-renewal agreement or checkbox must be clearly described, unchecked by default, and completed by an affirmative action before the purchase request is sent. Opening a purchase page, viewing or dismissing a notice, continuing after a timeout, or merely using the Application is not consent. Your purchase confirmation and any required affirmative checkbox authorize the purchase under the terms shown at that time. We may retain order, entitlement, and terms information needed to deliver the product, prevent duplicate grants, handle refunds, and comply with law, as described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Managing or cancelling a subscription

Use the cancellation method below for the channel that processed the purchase. For Google Play, open the Google Play Store using the Google account used for the purchase, choose Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select the PrismPop subscription, choose Cancel subscription, and follow the instructions. See Google Play’s subscription instructions.

For Apple, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select the PrismPop subscription, choose Cancel Subscription, and follow the instructions. See Apple’s subscription instructions. Cancelling normally stops a future renewal; it does not automatically refund an earlier charge. Access after cancellation depends on the store, product, and applicable law.

For a third-party payment channel, contact prismpop8893@gmail.com to request cancellation and provide the order identifier, account information, and purchase channel needed to locate the subscription. Do not send full payment-card details or passwords.
